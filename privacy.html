VELVET TIP

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Velvet Tip ("we", "us") is an app that lets you preview nail designs on a photo of your own hand. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can to make the app work.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account ID. When you first open the app we create an anonymous account so your try-ons and purchases can be linked to you. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your hand photo. When you try on a design, the photo is sent from your device to our server and to Google's Gemini API, which edits the nails and returns the result. We do not keep your original hand photo on our servers; it stays on your device.
  • Inspiration photos and text. If you add an inspiration image or describe a design, it is sent the same way, only to create your result, and is not stored on our servers.
  • Your results ("Looks"). The edited images are saved on your device and, so you can get them back on a new device, in private cloud storage linked to your anonymous ID. Only you can access them.
  • Skin tone estimate. To show how well a design suits you, we send a small copy of your hand photo for analysis once and store only the result (for example "warm, light") on your device.
  • Usage records. For each try-on we record the time, the design name, shape, length and whether it succeeded. We use this to run the service, enforce free limits and prevent abuse.
  • Purchase status. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We learn whether your anonymous account has an active subscription through RevenueCat; we never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We don't show ads or track you across other apps and websites.
  • We don't use your photos to train our own models.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — hosts our server, database and image storage.
  • Google (Gemini API) — creates the edited images and the skin tone estimate. Google processes the images under its API terms.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status.
  • Apple — processes payments through the App Store.

How long we keep it

  • Results stay until you delete them. Deleting a Look in the app removes its image from our storage.
  • Usage records are kept while your account exists so limits keep working.
  • Hand and inspiration photos are not stored on our servers after your result is created.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete any Look from the app at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete all data linked to your account, or for a copy of it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights, and you can contact your local data protection authority.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

Children

Velvet Tip is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU), and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and stored images are private to your account. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
